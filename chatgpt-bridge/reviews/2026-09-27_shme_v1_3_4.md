## 1. Opposite-direction continuity

### 1.1 Correction of the V1.3.3 measurement

V1.3.3 section 3 reported "12-17 cm pelvis / 20-26 cm foot jumps in one frame" at database switches. Re-measured in the
mesh frame (pelvis and feet relative to the root socket, per 33 ms tick): at sprint the pelvis travels ~14 cm per
frame just by running, and the swing foot 20-25 cm, so those numbers were world-space travel. Local-frame deltas at
the switch frames are 0.5-2.5 cm (pelvis) and inside the normal swing envelope (feet): **there are no one-frame
skeletal teleports at database switches; the Motion Matching blend works.** The player-visible defect is what the
thrash table shows: the chooser changes database two or three times inside 0.03-0.25 s on a direction change
(`Jog_Fwd > Jog_Fwd_Pivot > Jog_Fwd > Jog_Fwd_Pivot > Walk_Bwd > Jog_Rear` on a jog 180), each switch restarting the
search with the continuing pose invalidated, so the body re-phases repeatedly. The 150 deg "pelvis yaw jump" was the ABP
pelvis-yaw telemetry at the `Jog_Fwd_Pivot -> Jog_RearRight` switch (a rear-facing clip under a forward-facing actor).

### 1.3 Implementation: committed redirection episode + database hold

SHME planner (`SHM_UpdateRouting`): an episode starts when grounded, with intent, speed > `RedirectEpisodeMinSpeed`
120 and the velocity / desired-velocity angle > `RedirectEpisodeAngle` 50 deg; it latches the desired direction,
speed, support side and gait phase; it ends only when the velocity aligns within `RedirectAlignAngle` 25 deg after
`RedirectMinDwell` 0.2 s, at `RedirectMaxDwell` 0.7 s, on a genuinely new intent (> `RedirectNewIntentAngle` 45 deg from
the latched direction, which restarts the episode with the new latch), or when ground / intent / speed are lost
(`RedirectEnd` 1 aligned / 2 timeout / 3 new intent / 4 lost). ABP `Update_MotionMatching` entry
(`install_redirect_hold.py`): on the episode's first frame the AAMS chooser tree runs as before (its first response is
the bridge); from the second frame the node keeps that database (`SHM_HoldDB` latched from `CurrentSelectedDatabase`,
`SetDatabaseToSearch` with `DoNotInterrupt`) and the tree is skipped until the episode ends; family routes (recovery,
turn-in-place, start transfer, blocked) keep priority (hold only while `SHM_Family` = 0). No global change to
`InterruptOnDatabaseChangeAndInvalidateContinuingPose` (audited: 30 AAMS switch calls use it, two use
`InterruptOnDatabaseChange`, one `DoNotInterrupt`); the hold removes the re-searches instead of softening them.
No new bridge clips were authored: the AAMS pivot / rear / diagonal vocabulary that the chooser's first response picks
is the bridge (section 1.4 lists what it picked).

**Second build (chain I)**: the database hold alone did not cut the bursts (jog `v134r_dir_jog`: 34 switches / 20
bursts; the node kept re-selecting between near-equal pivot clips inside the held database every frame, e.g.
`CP2_Jog_Fwd_Pivot <-> DPP_Jog_Fwd_Pivot` on the -135 stage), so the episode now also owns the search: while
`SHM_RedirectHold` the node's `SearchThrottleTime` is 0.6 s (`install_redirect_throttle.py`; the chooser's first pick
plays through the episode), else the V1.3.1 binding. Two defects found by the per-frame episode trace and fixed before
the final measurement: (a) the snapshot's hold flag had its first operand unwired (a shared DSL binding consumed three
times connected only twice; rewritten with explicit getters), so the hold was never true on the first build; (b) the
episode ended as soon as speed fell under 30 cm/s during the pivot (`rdLost` speed clause), releasing the hold at the
most fragile moment; speed loss inside an episode no longer ends it (ground, obstacle and intent loss still do).

## 3. Grounded vertical camera (stairs)

Owner located (V1.3.3 said the profile stair terms were not the lever; this pass found why): `CT_UpdateCameraHeight`
tracks the capsule height correctly (response 8 / s, max lag 28 cm), but the skeleton does not stay at the capsule's
height on stairs: foot placement pulls the hips down onto the treads, so camera Z minus pelvis Z grows from the flat
77 cm to 100-134 cm walking up and 76-154 cm jogging up (`v133cam_s6`), with the horizontal distance unchanged
(160-170 cm). The camera is not lagging; it is too high over a body that hangs below its capsule. Fix (spec 27): a
body-based vertical target through the existing SHME pivot-offset pipe (`GR_SHM_PivotOffset`): the pelvis height
relative to the capsule, minus the rest offset (`CamBodyRestOffset`), critically damped at `CamBodyFreq` 3.5 rad/s
(gait bobbing at ~2 Hz attenuated, sustained stair offsets followed), grounded only, bounded by `CamBodyMax` 60 cm;
the air path (apex / landing) is untouched.

**Before / after** (`cam_frame.py` head elevation from the camera, flat = -5 deg; camera Z over pelvis Z):

| stage | before `v133cam_s6`: head elev mean / min, camZ-pelvisZ mean (max) | after `v134c_s6`: head elev mean / min, camZ-pelvisZ mean |
|---|---|---|
| walk stairs up | -12.6 / -23.2, 100 (134) | **-3.4 / -13.2, 72** |
| walk stairs down | -7.9 / -13.8, 85 (104) | -5.9 / -10.6, 79 |
| jog stairs up | -12.9 / -26.2, 104 (154) | **-4.4 / -15.6, 74** |
| jog stairs down | -6.1 / -12.0, 80 (96) | -6.4 / -11.0, 82 |

The 17-21 deg framing loss on the climbs is gone on average (within 2 deg of flat); transient minima remain at
-13 / -16 deg where the hips drop up to 61-85 cm below the capsule for a few frames (jog up; the spring's bound
`CamBodyMax` 60 and its 3.5 rad/s response limit the chase, by design: no bobbing). The body term measured on flat
ground read -6 to -10 cm with the initial rest offset of 8 cm (the pelvis sits ~1-2 cm above the capsule centre when
walking, lower at sprint), so `CamBodyRestOffset` was set to 2 cm after this run (the stairs x5 runs of section 7 use
it); flat framing with 8 cm was 4 cm lower than before (head elev -5.5 vs -6.9), i.e. the character slightly higher in
frame. The air path is untouched (apex / landing unchanged).


## 4. Cinematic camera language, gait response, body coupling

Implemented in the SHME camera function (`SHM_UpdateCameraSignals`), consumed through the rig's existing SHME pipes
(`GR_SHM_BoomScale`, `GR_SHM_ShoulderScale`, `GR_SHM_PivotOffset`), no new rig controller:

- **MovementCameraIntensity** (spec 38): 0.6 x speed / 490 + 0.2 x |acceleration| / 800 + 0.1 x braking demand + 0.1 x
  redirection demand / 2, filtered at 0.3 s, grounded only. Measured: walk 0.14, jog 0.37, sprint 0.58 (`v134t_s3`).
- **Boom**: the profile's per-gait arm (170 / 184) stays the base; the intensity adds up to `CamIntensityBoom` 6 %
  continuously (boom scale 1.06 walk / 1.07 jog / 1.09 sprint), so walk is the closest and calmest, sprint the widest,
  with no hard switch. FOV stays the profile's 62 / 64 (no FOV-only change; the lens A/B of spec 18-19 was NOT done).
- **Acceleration lag / braking catch-up** (spec 42): a critically damped spring on the pivot along travel,
  `CamAccelCarry` 8 cm at 800 cm/s^2 (measured -3.6 .. +3.4 cm on starts and stops), added to the V1.2 brake carry.
- **Turn lag** (spec 43): a lateral pivot spring driven by the lateral component of dV (`CamTurnCarry` 6 cm at
  400 cm/s), measured -3.8 .. +2.7 cm on diagonals and strafes: the body redirects first, the camera follows by a
  few centimetres a few frames later; no look latency (the control rotation is untouched).
- **Shoulder side by travel** (section 2), **body height** (section 3), **enclosed-space rise** (section 5).
- Cinematic depth / lens: not changed in this pass beyond the DOF baseline (character sharp, f/8, focal 175 cm);
  the intended lens A/B (longer-feeling lens + camera moved back) and the dynamic focus distance (spec 19, 23) were NOT
  built. Depth from geometry (parallax, foreground overlap) is the house itself; no camera-side change claims it.

## 6. True-FPS capture (spec 46-47)

Tried in the spec's order. (1) **Take Recorder**: available (`TakeRecorderBlueprintLibrary`, `TakeRecorderPanel`,
`TakeRecorderActorSource`); the harness spawns a cine camera that mirrors the gameplay camera each tick, creates a
project-owned level sequence (`/Game/GraceRanger/SHME/QA/Takes/LS_<label>`), sets the panel up for it at 60 fps and adds
the pawn and the cine camera as actor sources - three attempts (`add_source_for_actor`, `add_source` + target before
and after the panel setup) all failed at `start_recording` with "no active or valid sources to record" (the panel does not
accept sources added by script while PIE is running); the render side (`render_take.py`: camera-cut track bound to the
recorded camera + `SequencerTools.render_movie` with the AVI / PNG protocols at a fixed 60 fps) is written and untested
because no take was produced. (2) **Movie Render Queue**: the plugin is not enabled in the project (no
`MoviePipelineQueueSubsystem`); enabling it is a project-config change and a restart, not done. (3) **Viewport recording /
fixed-timestep**: the editor was launched without `-benchmark`, so the game tick is wall-time bound; per-frame
screenshots stall the frame to ~4 game fps.

**Delivered fallback**: PIE at time dilation 0.1 with a screenshot every 0.33 s of wall time = one frame every
0.035 s of game time (28.6 game-fps sampling, 243 frames per 8.4 s clip), assembled with true frame delays:
`v134_30fps_jog_180.gif` (jog forward, 180 input reversal, forward), `v134_30fps_sprint_LR.gif` (sprint L -> R -> forward),
both through the gameplay camera on the final build. Honest limits: the simulation ran at a 10x smaller tick than real
play (motion matching searches per game-second differ), the sampling is 28.6 not 30 fps, and GIF is not a video codec.
The spec's "30/60 true game FPS video" is therefore **NOT met**; the opposite-direction (spec 48), diagonal (49) and
cinematic-depth (50) video sets were not produced.

## 7. Diagonal-stop bisect, 10 FPS, stairs determinism

**Diagonal-stop bisect (spec 52): first causal change found for the tap stops.** Step 1: the start suite with SHME
entirely off (`v134_s10_off`, 30 FPS) shows the same diagonal stops as with SHME on (l_45 sustained 7.6 / 7.6, r_45
