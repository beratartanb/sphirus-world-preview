## 2. Turn-in-place left / right symmetry (bisection)

Mirrored schedule +45 / 0 / -45 / 0 / +90 / 0 / -90 / 0 (3 s holds), one change per run:

| run | +45 foot travel | -45 | +90 | -90 | selection |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: counter on, turn route + unwind disabled (`TipEnterAngle` 999) | 11.2 | 14.9 | 27.2 | 27.8 | idle only |
| B: V1.3.2 final | 10.6 | 17.0 | 37.1 | 37.2 | `Turn_045_R` x27 / `Turn_045_L` x20 / `Turn_045_R` x63 / `Turn_045_L` x25 |

The internal values mirror in sign (counter -45 / +45, clips L / R by side); the V1.3.2 "asymmetry" (right 9-10 cm,
left 24-27 cm) came from the sequential schedule (each left step followed the 180 -> 0 return with residual counter /
hold state), not from a coordinate convention. Conventions, documented once: control yaw and actor yaw are world yaw
(+ = clockwise seen from above, UE left-handed), mesh component yaw = actor yaw - 90 (rest offset), `SHM_RootCounter`
= -(actor yaw change) applied as `RotateRootBone.Yaw` (+ rotates the root the same way as a + actor yaw), root-yaw
telemetry = root socket yaw - actor yaw + 90, "left" = negative yaw everywhere. Remaining: both directions still drift
27-37 cm on a direct 0 -> 90 step (the leg twist reaches the IK limit before the 35 deg turn fires and the turn clip's
own stepping counts in the metric); see section 7 / 1x review.

## 4. Diagonal lean: skeleton vs camera

Pelvis / spine roll (world, + = right side down, from the pelvis / spine_03 right vectors; a constant bone-axis
offset cancels between mirrored stages):

| stage | pelvis roll | spine roll | camera lateral offset (+ = right of the travel direction) | camera behind |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sprint_FL | -10.7 | -27.8 | **-95 cm** | 163 cm |
| sprint_FR | -10.5 | -27.3 | **+147 cm** | 114 cm |
| left (jog strafe) | +3.7 | +0.8 | -145 cm | 72 cm |
| right (jog strafe) | -5.1 | -5.4 | +124 cm | -31 cm |
| sprint approach (straight) | -7.1 / -7.6 | -20.7 / -19.2 | +39 cm | 168 cm |

The skeleton does NOT lean into either diagonal: forward-left and forward-right carry the same roll (-10.7 vs -10.5),
which is the sprint clips' baseline roll (the straight approach has -7); the jog strafes are roughly mirrored (+3.7 /
-5.1). What differs by direction is the CAMERA: with look-driven facing the camera keeps the look axis while the body
travels 45 deg off it, so it sits ~1-1.5 m to the SIDE of the travel line (left of travel on FL, right of travel on
FR) and much closer behind on FR / right (114 / -31 cm) than on FL / left (163 / 72 cm). An off-axis, asymmetric view
of a symmetric body reads as a tilt toward one side - the "opposite-side lean" is camera composition, not animation
(spec 21). Not changed in this pass: the shoulder / lateral framing rule would have to be made symmetric with respect to
the travel direction (camera skill owner); no lean value was reversed.

## 5. Recovery side commitment

**Change** (ABP `SHM_Snapshot`, two DSL lines, no new subsystem): the recovery route used the component's instantaneous
`RecoverySide` / support-load confidence every frame, so the database could re-target mid-step (V1.3.2 shoves: side and
database flipped inside one recovery). V1.3.3 latches both per episode: `SHM_CommitSide` is taken on the first confident
frame of a recovery episode (family 1 / 6) and held until the episode ends (`SHM_CommitLatched`, `SHM_RecPrev`), and
`SHM_SideConfident` is now the latch itself while in recovery (before the first confident frame the combined
`PSD_SHM_Recovery_v131` is searched, as in V1.3.2). An earlier variant that committed on entry (before any confident frame)
routed the shoves through the combined database (V1.3.2-like 20 % / 10 %) and was replaced by the first-confident-frame
latch; the chain-C build still let the database fall back to the combined set when confidence dipped (9/10 frames on one
database); chain D (final build) holds it.

Chain C (same latch on the side, confidence not latched): forced 18 episodes, side constant 18 / 18 but database constant

forced route, 20 % / 10 % on the shoves.

**Honesty note on the metric.** `side_agreement.py`'s original number (component `RecoverySide` vs clip plant foot, now
printed as "plant-foot agreement") reads 20 % / 10 % / 43 % on the shoves and 95 % on the forced route: the raw side

with `SupportLoadR - SupportLoadL` above 0.3; the 1x GIF `v133_1x_shove_strong.gif` shows a single plausible step; no
human ground truth).

The user-observed symptom is confirmed and quantified: climbing stairs drops the head by 17-21 deg below the flat framing
(descending 9 deg), i.e. the character sits far lower in the frame while grounded on stairs. The GR profile's stair
parameters (response doubled, lag halved) did NOT move it (differences within 1-4 deg, one stage worse), so the lever is
not in the profile's stair-continuity term; it is in `CT_UpdateCameraHeight`'s grounded lerp / unconstrained-height
path (`GR_FilteredCameraWorldZ`, `GR_CameraHeightUnconstrained`), which was not modified in this pass. The profile was
reverted to 8 / 28. The stairs slide regression during the A/B: `jog_stairs_down_setup` 47.8 again (setup anomaly,
nondeterministic 22.6 / 5.4 / 5.4 / 47.8 across the pass - the teleport guard reduces but does not close it).

**Gait camera language**: the profile already distinguishes gaits (FOV 62 -> sprint 64, arm 170 -> sprint 184,
follow 32 walk / 42 run, turn catch-up gain 3); no change was made in this pass and no per-gait feel measurement was
built beyond boom / FOV values (spec 41-46: not done).

**Depth of field** (`set_dof.py`, `BP_PBI_Player` camera component post-process: f/8, focal distance 175 cm = the walk
