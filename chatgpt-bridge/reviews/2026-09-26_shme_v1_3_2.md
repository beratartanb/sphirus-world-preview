## 3. Turn-in-place: pose ownership and root cause

Ownership on every frame of the test: the GraceRanger rig (`BP_GR_Player.GR_UpdateRig`, `LookDrivenLocomotion = true` in
`DA_GR_LocomotionBehavior`) rotates the ACTOR toward the control yaw (RInterpTo_Constant), standing or not;
`Get_OffsetRootRotationMode` returns Release while idle (Accumulate only while moving and not look-driven, Interpolate
with the arms revision), so the mesh follows the actor rigidly; the SHME idle takeover (`BlendListByBool_5` true side)
owns the pose with a frozen stance (snapshot + Ranger idle) and the Motion Matching branch is not relevant, so the AAMS
`Stand_TurnInPlace` vocabulary is never searched.
Root cause, three parts, all confirmed by the frame data: (1) **the standing body is turned by the actor rotation** (a
statue on a turntable: 10-13 cm of planted-foot travel per 30-45 deg step, 118 cm over the sweep) because the root yaw
offset is released in idle; (2) **no turn-in-place motion exists in the idle path** (the takeover hides the MM and the
offset never reaches the 50 deg `ShouldTurnInPlace` threshold anyway); (3) **two stance families**: when the takeover
releases or re-enters (velocity, `CP_IdleReady`, stop settle), the pose jumps between the Ranger idle (feet 28 cm apart,
no stagger) and the AAMS idle / turn clips (feet 40 cm apart, 35 cm stagger, 22 deg pelvis yaw difference) - section 4.
Longer blend times or camera lag cannot fix any of the three.

Fix (V1.3.2, `install_tip_rootmode.py`, `install_tip_layer.py`, snapshot patch):
- `Get_OffsetRootRotationMode` = Accumulate while idle and `SHM_TipEnabled` (moving behaviour unchanged): the actor keeps
  following the look (camera / facing ownership untouched, V1.3.1 camera preserved), the mesh root keeps the feet planted;
  small look changes (< `TipEnterAngle` 45 deg) are absorbed by the root offset + AAMS look-at layer (head / torso), no
  full-body pose change.
- `SHM_Snapshot` computes the root yaw offset (root socket yaw - actor yaw) and a hysteresis turn state (`SHM_TurnActive`:
  enter > 45 deg, exit < 8 deg after `TipMinTime` 0.3 s); while active the takeover releases (`SHM_IdleWait`), the stance
  hold releases, the CP stop latch releases and the MM (unthrottled, V1.3.1) searches `Stand_TurnInPlace`; the turn end
  re-enters idle so a fresh stance snapshot is taken from the post-turn stance.
- Stance identity: the takeover's non-hold pose is now LayeredBoneBlend(base = cached `MainStates` = the MM output
  (AAMS idle / turn clip, lower body), spine_01+ = Ranger idle). The lower body therefore always comes from one family
  (AAMS clips, or the snapshot of an AAMS stop stance) and the Ranger upper body stays through idle -> turn -> idle.
**What the retests forced (each step is a rerun with one change):** (a) the accumulate-while-idle change to
`Get_OffsetRootRotationMode` had no effect: that function is dead (the layer's OffsetRootBone has a literal mode);
(b) enabling the AAMS `TurnInPlace_Layer` OffsetRootBone under SHME's flag had no effect either: the root yaw offset
stayed 0 through 180 deg of look yaw with the MM idle owning the pose (`v132tip_exp`) - in this project that node does
not accumulate at all; (c) SHME therefore owns the standing root yaw: `SHM_RootCounter` (root-space counter of the actor's
yaw change while standing, unwound at `TipUnwindRate` 90 deg/s while the turn clip plays, released at `TipMoveRelease`
when movement starts) applied by a `RotateRootBone` on the final pose path (before the `ToLayers` cache), and a route
that sends the MM to `PSD_Dense_Stand_TurnInPlace` while `SHM_TurnActive` (continuing pose invalidated). The sign, the
node placement and the feet-IK interaction were each measured: a wrong sign doubles the foot travel; the first placement
(after `RotateRootBone_0`) sat in a branch the idle path never evaluates; toggling the AAMS foot placement off pops the
feet by 10-20 cm on every toggle, so foot placement stays on and keeps the planted feet in world.

stage-2 build uses 35):
