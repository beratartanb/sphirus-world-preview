## 2. Ground-driven curved running (20-30 deg)

**Matrix** (`v136d_*`: smooth look steering of 15 / 25 / 35 deg over 0.5 s, left and right, jog and sprint, plus walk 25;
`curve_report.py` over onset -0.2 .. +2.0 s): velocity heading change, peak lateral acceleration, support at onset,
redirect demand, and every foot plant (contact rising edge) with the plant-to-plant direction against the velocity
heading at that time - a plant train that keeps the old heading while the velocity turns is the "carried through the arc"
look even when the trajectory is right.

| case | heading change | peak lateral accel (cm/s2) | redirect demand | plant direction vs velocity heading, per plant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| jog R15 | 13 | 121 | 0.12 | L -7, R +3, L -8, R +3, L -5, R +7 |
| jog R25 | 27 | 200 | 0.20 | L -9, R -6, **L -10**, R +7, L -6, R +3 |
| jog L25 | -23 | 198 | 0.20 | L -2, L +5, R +3 (plants sparse in the edge detector) |
| jog R35 | 34 | 274 | 0.28 | R -5, **L -15**, R +6, L -5, R +3 |
| sprint R15 | 16 | 191 | 0.21 | L -5, R +2, L -1 |
| sprint R25 | 26 | **312** | **0.35** | L -10, L +1 (the second outside plant already aligned) |
| sprint R35 | 33 | 426 | 0.47 | (plants sparse) |
| walk R25 | 22 | 93 | 0.09 | R +2, L -5, R +1, L -1 (the walk aligns its plants within one step) |

Speed scaling (spec 13-14): the same 25 deg command produces 200 cm/s2 at jog and 312 at sprint (0.2 g vs 0.32 g;
centripetal v^2 / r with the wider sprint arc), redirect demand 0.20 vs 0.35, and the first outside plant lags by the same
~10 deg at both speeds - the ground reaction demand scales with speed as it should; what does not scale is the visible
support expression, which is the same clip family at both speeds.

Reading: the trajectory curves as expected (27 deg for the 25 deg command, lateral acceleration 200 cm/s2 = 0.2 g at
300 cm/s, i.e. a ~11 deg physical lean); the plants DO rotate into the new heading, but the OUTSIDE (left) foot on a
right curve plants 9-15 deg behind the velocity heading while the inside foot leads by 3-7 deg: the outside foot keeps
some of the old heading for one or two steps, which is the residual "slightly slippery" cue. The redirect demand stays
0.12-0.28 (below any episode / bridge), so this is inside continuous running, as the spec requires.

**What this pass adds**: a curve-lean coupling - the component's lateral acceleration (`LatAccel`, new) enters the PBI
severity the rig already consumes (`CurveLeanGain` 0.5 x |lateral accel| / 400), so the torso / pelvis lean scales with
the physical inward acceleration (0.25 severity at the jog 25, 0.34 at 35, ~0 on straight running), never with the input
angle; together with the coupling fade (section 6) it is the only channel SHME has into the pose at steady gait.
**Not done**: the outside-foot support expression at the plant (knee compression, lateral loading, next-plant rotation
into the new heading) needs foot placement / clip authoring that SHME does not own; the plant-lag metric above is the
acceptance instrument for it. Curved-run hard acceptance (spec 18): NOT met; measured and instrumented.

## 4. Stop -> settle -> idle

**Before** (`stop_report.py`, protocol-less stops on the V1.3.5 build): walk stop `v136b_stops`: speed < 10 at 0.44 s
after release, the stop clip (`SHM_A_CP2_Jog_Fwd_Stop`) plays, the idle takeover (stance snapshot + Ranger upper body)
engages at **1.09 s while the feet are still moving until 1.30 s** - the takeover arrives before the final plant has
settled, which is the abrupt stop -> idle; jog stop `v136b_stops_jog`: speed < 10 at 0.67 s, last foot motion 1.19 s,
takeover 1.30 s (in order).

**Tried and reverted**: a quiet-support criterion (`SettleQuiet` = COM speed under 12 cm/s for 0.35 s) that held the
idle takeover until the body was quiet. It did move the walk takeover after the last foot motion (1.60-1.64 s vs
1.20 s), but the gap it opened is filled by the AAMS stop search / raw idle re-planting the feet: the start suite's stops
skated (max 7.6 -> 16.7, then 24.1 cm; stops 0.6-3.6 -> 8-15 cm) and the standing idle swayed like the raw AAMS idle
(roll std 1.0 -> 3.9 / 5.4). In this pipeline the stance-snapshot takeover IS the settle (it freezes the stopped stance
and layers the quiet upper body); delaying it is wrong. Reverted (final build `v136k_*`: starts max 7.6 = V1.3.5, walk stop takeover 1.14 s, last foot motion 1.31 s - the V1.3.5 order). The remaining
"abrupt" cue at the walk stop is therefore the takeover arriving ~0.1-0.2 s before the last foot settles, and its fix
belongs in the takeover's own blend (a foot-quiet condition inside the takeover, not a delay of it) - open. No settle
clips authored.

## 5. Opposing-direction expression, sprint support commitment

**Momentum expression** (spec 37-41): not addressed beyond the bridge vocabulary; the V1.3.5 bridges were not

## 6. Diagonal lean, standing turns, sway (audits on the current build)

**Jog lean re-measured on V1.3.5** (`v135t_s3`, mean pelvis / spine roll, + = right side down): jog straight -6.6 /

**Standing look turns re-measured** (`v136b_tip`: +-15 / 30 / 45 / 60 / 90 / 180, 2.5 s holds): 15 and 30 deg stay
planted (foot travel 0.0-1.0 cm, no clip change); 45 plays `Stand_Turn_045_R` (one step, 10 cm), 60 no clip but 10 cm

**Sway** (std over the stage, `v135t_s3`): idle pelvis roll 0.96 deg, spine 1.04, pelvis lateral 1.5 cm (calm); walk
pelvis roll 4.1, spine 6.0, lateral 1.2 cm; jog 2.7 / 1.9; camera-indoor idle 2.9 / 2.5. The walk's spine roll (std 6
deg, i.e. ~+-10 deg peaks) is the visible sway; idle is already low-energy. Attribution: walk spine roll std 2.6 with
SHME off and 2.7 with body coupling off vs 6.0 with coupling on - the sway is added through the PBI coupling, although
SHME's severity is exactly 0 at steady walk: feeding that 0 replaces the PBI's own heuristic and doubles the roll.
Fix (this pass): the coupling weight now fades to the PBI heuristic when SHME has no event (`SHM_BodyBlend` x
clamp(severity / deadband)); result (`v136c_s3`): walk spine roll std **6.0 -> 2.7**, pelvis 4.1 -> 2.0 (the SHME-off
level), jog unchanged (2.8 / 1.9). The first version also faded at rest and let the PBI heuristic drive the idle (idle
std back to 3.8 / 5.3), so the fade is gated on motion. **Final build (`v136k_s3`, settle gate reverted)**: walk 4.25 / 5.97, idle 1.44 / 1.80,
jog 2.4 / 1.9 - i.e. the walk sway is back at the V1.3.5 level. The 2.0 / 2.7 walk figure of the intermediate builds
came with the settle gate, not with the fade: the walk stage starts from the stance-snapshot takeover, and its layered
upper body blends out over the first steps; with the gate the walk started from the raw idle instead. The walk sway
therefore tracks the takeover-to-walk transition (the Ranger idle layer bleeding into the first steps), which is the
lever for a later pass (a faster release of the layered upper body on movement onset); the coupling fade stays (it is
principled and neutral on this metric). Idle: SHME's stance-snapshot idle already calms the
AAMS idle from 3.9 / 5.5 to 1.0 / 1.0 deg std.

## 8. Exertion

**State** (`Exertion`, 0..1, component): work rate = (speed / 300)^2 + 0.4 x braking demand + 0.6 x redirection demand
(normalised) + 0.01 x climb speed + 0.8 while a recovery family is active, halved when airborne; the state rises toward
half the work rate with a 25 s time constant and falls with 40 s at rest (walk ~0.17 work -> ~0.08 target, jog ~1.0 ->
0.5, sprint ~2.7 -> capped 1.0); no gameplay effect. **Consumers in this pass**: the camera intensity boom term
(+30 % of exertion into the intensity, i.e. up to +2 % boom when tired) - subtle by design; breathing amplitude, shoulder
relaxation, arm-swing efficiency and the settle length (spec 57) were NOT connected: the ABP has no SHME-owned breathing or
upper-body relaxation layer to drive, and adding one is source / graph work beyond this pass. Telemetry `exr` / `exw`.
Measured: 30 s sprint (`v136c_exert_sprint30`) 0 -> 0.31 at 12 s, 0.51 at 20 s, 0.65 at 28 s, peak 0.70, decaying
after the stop; a fresh 3 s walk (`v136c_exert_fresh`) peaks at 0.011. The state is present and bounded; its visible
consequence in the body is NOT delivered in this pass (no breathing / posture consumer exists to drive), only the subtle
camera term.
