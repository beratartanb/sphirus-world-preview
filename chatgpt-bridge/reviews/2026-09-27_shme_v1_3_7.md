## 1. Real-map running freeze

**Exact reproduction** (spec 7-8). Production level, PIE, the real pawn / camera / terrain (no QA floor, no protocol actor).
W held for 130 s at jog and at sprint; mouse yaw swept left/right continuously (sine sweeps, amplitude cycling 15/25/40/30/20/35
deg, 1.2 s period) with a slow wandering drift (+-8 deg/s alternating every 15 s) so the path crosses open lawn, forest,
slopes and obstacles. Four starts: beside the house's south wall (house-adjacent), the west slope, the north lawn, toward the
pond. 8 runs x 130 s. Telemetry per tick: Motion Matching database / clip / clip time, CP owners, idle / wait, family,
redirect episode / hold / held database, blocked, environment clearances, speed, input acceleration and direction, actor
and control yaw, max walk speed, ground friction, active montage.

**What the freeze is.** Not a clip running off its end under a moving capsule (0 such frames in 1040 s, 0 `None`
selections). The character *stops and freezes with W held*: 20 episodes in 1040 s (22.6 s with the skeleton still,
32.4 s stopped with input held, 31.4 s with the facing locked while the camera moved). Three owners, all in the pawn's
real parent Blueprint `/Game/GraceRanger/BP_GR_Player` and SHME:

| owner | mechanism | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `GR_UpdateCoreContact` + EventGraph core-contact bump | brushing a trunk / rock / the house corner at speed plays a bump montage `A_CP2_Bump_*` (2.83-3.0 s, root motion from montages): the stagger ends at 1.1-1.45 s, the remaining 1.4-1.9 s is a static recovery that ignores input; the bump released early only when the stick pointed away from the obstacle, which W + camera sweeps around the impact heading never does | bump montages 2.73-2.77 s at exactly the stuck episodes (house corner 3.9 s, 20.6 s, 56.3 s) |
| `GR_Impl_CP_PhysicalRedirect` | the CMC rotation rate is zeroed and the actor is turned manually only between 20 and 3000 cm/s and without a montage: stopped against an obstacle, the facing never follows the camera, so the character keeps pushing into the obstacle | actor yaw frozen for seconds while the control yaw swept 60-100 deg (e.g. 121.8 deg for 9 s beside a tree) |
| SHME `Blocked` | the blocked probe follows the swept input direction, hits / misses the obstacle every few frames, and the ABP idle takeover engaged in between | blocked flickering true / false every ~0.5 s on the hillside |

**Fix at the owners** (no timers, no periodic resets):
1. `GR_UpdateCoreContact`: the bump montage is released once its stagger has played out (`Montage_GetPosition >=
   GR_ContactReleaseTime`, 1.25 s) *while movement input is held*; the static tail plays only when the player lets go.
   Existing releases (steering away, airborne, crouch, aim, interaction) unchanged; re-arm logic unchanged.
2. `GR_Impl_CP_PhysicalRedirect`: a stall timer (`GR_StallTime`: input held and the capsule below 20 cm/s) lets the
   look-driven facing follow the camera once stalled for 0.35 s; starts are untouched (they pass 20 cm/s in ~0.05 s).
3. SHME `SHM_UpdateRouting`: blocked hysteresis - once blocked, the state holds while the body stays stopped with intent.

A first install went into the Sandbox copy `/Game/GraceRanger/Experimental/BodyInertia/Sandbox/BP_GR_Player` (not the
pawn's parent: the asset registry shows `BP_PBI_Player` -> `/Game/GraceRanger/BP_GR_Player`); it had no runtime effect,
was detected from the new telemetry, and was reverted (original links restored, the 11 added nodes and the variable
removed, saved).

**120 s result** (same 8 runs after the fix, `stuck_report.py` / `freeze_report.py`):

|---|---|---|

## 2. Gait speed calibration

**BEFORE** (`v137c_base_*`, flat QA floor, 10 s per direction, `gait_report.py`): walk 132, jog 300, sprint 490 cm/s;
planted balls slid BACKWARD every step (walk -7.4, jog -7.7, sprint -3.2 cm per plant); the feet "supported" 11-12 %
more speed than the capsule at walk / jog and 5 % at sprint.

**ROOT CAUSE**. The capsule does not outrun the feet on straights: the FootPlacement plant lock (PivotAroundBall) held
each planted ball in world space and then dragged it backward under the moving body. A/B on identical runs: PBI off and
pelvis lag off changed nothing; FootPlacement off removed the drag but let the feet float on slopes (56-58 % of stance
frames).

**ACTUAL CHANGE**. Per-loop curve `Disable_FootLock` = 1 on the 33 project-owned steady loops (the plant lock stays for
starts, stops, pivots and terrain), `Enable_OrientationWarping` = 1 on the same loops, and a dynamic re-lock while the
body turns (ModifyCurve before the FeetIK layer, alpha `SHM_FootLockTurn`). Speeds are unchanged (walk 132 / jog 300 /
sprint 490; side scale 0.85, backward 245): with the drag gone the feet support 0-5 % MORE distance than the capsule
travels at jog and sprint, so the measurement does not support slowing the character.

**ASSETS**: 33 loops (`LocomotionBehavior`, `Continuity`, `ContactLocomotion`, `CorePerformance`, `RearRevision`);
`ABP_GR_MotionMatchingBase` (ModifyCurve node, `SHM_Snapshot`).

## 3. Curved running

**BEFORE**. V1.3.6 reported a 9-15 deg outside-foot plant lag. Measured at the start of this pass on the RENDERED body
(steering run: W held, camera sine sweeps 15-40 deg, 1.2 s period, `curve_report.py`): jog outside-foot step lag 14.0 deg
with 3.5-4.4 cm lateral planted slide per plant; sprint 15.4 deg with 6.4 cm.

**ROOT CAUSE**. The project steady loops had no `Enable_OrientationWarping` curve (warp alpha 0): when travel and facing
differ the feet kept the clip's straight-ahead plants and were carried sideways; with the plant lock removed for the
straight-line drag the planted foot also slid.

**ACTUAL CHANGE**. Orientation-warp curve on the 33 loops; dynamic plant lock `SHM_FootLockTurn` (rises within 0.05 s as
|yaw rate| / 45 deg/s or |lateral accel| / 250 cm/s^2 grows, releases over 0.6 s) blended into the loops'
`Disable_FootLock` through a ModifyCurve before the FeetIK layer, so the plant holds while the body turns over it.

**AFTER** (`v137v_steer_*`, rendered body; toe = foot -> ball heading at touchdown vs the capsule tangent):

| sprint | lags 7.5 deg | leads 22.8 deg | 14.7 deg | 5.7 / 1.7 cm |
