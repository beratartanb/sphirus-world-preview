# SHME V1.2: human motor control + biomechanics + animation reconstruction (2026-09-26)

**Acceptance: PARTIAL V1.2** (technical PASS on the measured targets A, D, E, F and the motor-model signals; B and C are
partial; real-time player review, physical devices and the real house remain NOT_TESTED by a person).

Continues V1.1 in place (nothing rebuilt). Every number below is from the PIE QA harness with injected input at 30 FPS
unless stated; "slide" is the 09-21 foot-slide proxy (max planted-foot travel in cm while the foot-speed curve says
planted). Numbers and appearance are reported separately (section 13).

## 1. The human motor model (what the machine now knows)

If this were a humanoid machine with a human skeleton, the answer V1.2 gives it is: *know where your mass is, know which
foot carries it, know what the ground can give you, decide what you want, and let the body execute with the delays and
recoveries a body has*. Concretely the SHME component now carries, every PostPhysics tick:

| Quantity | Kind | Source / equation |
|---|---|---|
| Segment masses, COM ratios | data | Dempster via Winter 2009 Table 4.1 (`DA_SHM_Anthropometry_Default`) |
| Whole-body COM, COM velocity, COM acceleration | approximation | mass-weighted bone positions, filtered (`ComVelFilterTau`) |
| Foot contact L/R with hysteresis, contact confidence | approximation | bone speed + height vs the support plane |
| Support side, double support, support region | approximation | confidence margin, capsule segment foot->foot |
| **Gait phase** | approximation | 0 double support, 1 L stance/R swing, 2 R stance/L swing, 3 flight; dwell hysteresis `GaitMinDwell` 0.05 s; heel-strike / toe-off events, L cycle time, cadence |
| **Support load L/R** | approximation | COM projected on the inter-foot axis, weighted by contact confidence (sums to 1 on the ground) |
| **Centre of pressure** | approximation (LIP) | p = x_com - (h/g) x_com'' clamped to the support segment (double support) or the support-foot disc |
| Capture point, capture error, stability margin | approximation | LIP: omega = sqrt(g/h), CP = p_com + v_com/omega (Hof 2005) |
| Linear momentum | measured x data | p = m v (kg m/s) |
| **Yaw angular momentum / torque** | approximation | L_z = m r_g^2 omega, tau_z = I alpha with r_g = 0.14 m (`RadiusOfGyrationYawM`) |
| **Braking impulse** | approximation | J = m (v_peak - v) while braking |
| Landing impulse, severity, recovery time | approximation | J = m |v_z,impact|, severity from soft/hard speeds |
| Traction, braking, turn, reversal, recovery demand | heuristic | |F_t| <= mu F_n normalisation; d = v^2/(2a); a_lat = v omega |
| **Anticipatory postural adjustment** | approximation | intent onset from rest -> CoP shift of `ApaShiftCm` 3 cm opposite to the step for `ApaDuration` 0.15 s (signal; not yet consumed) |
| **PhysicalMotionIntent** | heuristic | see section 4 |

Voluntary intent is immediate (input -> `HasIntent`, `DesiredDir`); physical execution is delayed and bounded (filters,
dwell, recovery timers, reach limits). Nothing here is measured physics; the labels above are honest.

## 2. Equations and units

All internal state is UE units: cm, s, kg, degrees on the wire. Centralised constants on `DA_SHM_Response_Default`:
`Gravity` 980 cm/s^2, `CmPerMeter` 100 (used for every SI conversion: momentum, impulse, angular momentum). Time
constants are seconds and every filter uses alpha = 1 - exp(-dt/tau) (frame-rate independent; verified at 10/30/60 FPS).

## 3. Approximations (and what they are not)

- COM: 16 segments, no head/hand offsets, no clothing. Good to a few cm.
- CoP: no pressure sensor exists; the LIP relation gives the point the body *would* need under it. It is clamped to the
  support so it never leaves the feet.
- Angular momentum is rigid-body yaw only (arms/legs relative motion ignored).
- Support load is a geometric projection, not force plates.
- Gait phase relies on the animation's foot contact; FootPlacement corrections are included because the bones are read
  after IK.

## 4. PhysicalMotionIntent and the selection-cost concept

Fields on the component (telemetry `pmi/pmid/pmiu/pmic`): `PMI_Type` (0 idle, 1 start, 2 continue, 3 stop, 4 turn,
5 reversal, 6 recovery step, 7 airborne, 8 landing), `PMI_Direction` (signed deg, + left), `PMI_Urgency`
(max of traction, recovery and landing severity), `PMI_Cost` = traction + recovery + (1 - double support) x 0.5 when
committing + stance error/50. The cost is the *physical price of executing the intent now*; it is what a chooser or
database tag should weigh against animation-match cost. **It is computed and logged; it is not yet wired into the
chooser or PoseSearch tags** (section 7).

## 6. Target A: diagonal (and every other) stop slide

**Root cause (three layers, all measured):**
1. Every AAMS stop clip ends in the same wide staggered stand (L 30 cm fwd / 22 left, R 10 back / 28 right in the actor
   frame); the Ranger idle stance is feet-together (L 8/-13, R 7/15). The idle takeover (`BlendListByBool_5`, 0.2 s
   crossfade to `A_Ranger_Idle_Neutral_CWF`) therefore dragged both planted feet ~25 cm on every stop; diagonals were
   worst because their end stances are furthest from the idle.
2. The vendor FR/FL tails additionally slide a planted foot 12-20 cm flat with the foot-speed curve between the
   FootPlacement plant/unplant thresholds (the foot is released and re-locked to the old plant).
3. The run/jog stop clips do the same (6-31 cm), and FootPlacement's re-lock to the previous plant point pulls the foot back.

**Fix (animation first):**
- 23 project-owned corrected copies under `/Game/GraceRanger/SHME/Animations/Stops/SHM_*`: pelvis + legs held in local
  space from the frame after the last real step (FR_Lfoot 1.8 s, FR_Rfoot 2.0 s, FL_Rfoot 1.6 s, A_RR F_L 1.8 s,
  A_RR F_R 1.5 s, A_CP2 jog 1.77 s); the other 17 walk stops are verbatim copies so that direction competition is
  unchanged. Copies branch into the same project-owned databases (`Integrated/PSD_Dense_Stand_Walk_Stops`,
  `PSD_RR_Stop`, `PSD_CP2_JogStop`) with a -30 cost bias so they win ties with the vendor entries. Vendor assets untouched.
- **Stance-preserving idle** (Sandbox `ABP_GR_MotionMatchingBase`): on the idle-takeover entry edge the ABP saves a pose
  snapshot `SHM_StanceHold` and latches `SHM_IdleHold` after every real stop (speed peak > 30 cm/s); the idle pose is then
  LayeredBoneBlend(base = snapshot, spine_01+ = Ranger idle): torso idles, feet stay where they were planted. The latch
  is decided once per idle and kept until the next entry (clearing it while the takeover fades exposed the plain idle);
  the entry edge follows the takeover's own terms (no velocity, not falling, no core brake, no landing hold). Two
  stricter variants (ending the idle on any foot motion under a hold, and a 0.12 s rest dwell) were measured and
  rejected: they re-enter mid-step during starts and taps (suite 10 setups 7 -> 22 cm).
  A stance-error test against the Ranger stance (`StanceError`, `IdleStanceTolerance`) was tried first and dropped:
  the idle asset's frame after turns is neither the actor nor the mesh frame, and a wrong reference chose the
  crossfade at 2-4 cm measured error while the feet then slid 28 cm. It stays as telemetry. Flag `StanceIdleGateEnabled`.

## 7. Targets B, C, D

- **B (45/90/180 visible distinction):** PBI direction channels A/B on suite 5 (sprint 90/180 stages, `body_metrics.py`),
  gains DirectionCounterLean / PelvisYaw / ShoulderBalance at 0 / current (7/6/3) / x1.6 (11.2/9.6/4.8): torso lean,
  shoulder counter-yaw and wobble differ by < 2 cm / < 1 deg / < 0.05 between all three (90: lean_l 11-12 cm, cyaw 56-61 deg;
  180: lean_l 2.8 cm, cyaw 18-21 deg, wobble 0.2-0.65 in every variant). The visible 45/90/180 difference is entirely the
  animation selection (turn/pivot clips); the PBI direction channel cannot add a visible physically-derived consequence in
  this gain range and was left at the current values (no wobble added, nothing regressed). The physically-derived
  quantities that *should* separate the cases (yaw angular momentum, torque, braking impulse) are now computed and logged
  for a future selection-cost consumer. PARTIAL.
- **C (capture point -> recovery routing):** the capture point drives StepUrgency/RecommitDemand/RecoveryDemand and
  now `PMI_Type` 6 (recovery step) with direction and urgency; there is no recovery-step animation to route to (audit),
  so routing stops at the signal. PARTIAL: vocabulary gap, not a logic gap.
- **D (FootPlan -> IK):** measured planned-target vs actual touchdown error over every touchdown while moving
  (`target_error.py`): median 10.9 cm (suite 10), 5.5 cm when plan confidence > 0.5 and 12.6 cm when it is low;
  16-18 cm on jog/sprint. An IK pull would either move feet <= 5 cm (no visible benefit) or fight the animation by
  12+ cm. Decision: **no IK wiring**; the plan stays a planning/telemetry signal. Measured, closed.

## 8. Target E: settle side effects

The V1.1 capsule settle existed to stop the idle from re-centring the body onto a capsule that stopped 10-14 cm behind
the COM. With the stance-preserving idle the body no longer re-centres, so the settle has no remaining benefit and
still moves the capsule under planted feet: suite 10 with settle on vs off: +45 stops 4.3/6.7 vs 0.8/0.1, forward
sustained 5.6 vs 1.1; jog stop 3.4 vs 2.3; wall/stairs +2.2 cm in V1.1 came from 8.5-8.7 cm offsets. The settle now
carries an expected-benefit gate (`benefit = not (StanceIdleGateEnabled and IdleHold)`, `SettleMinOffset` 10) and its
default is **off** (`SettleEnabled=false`); turning it on reproduces V1.1 behaviour.

## 9. Target F: camera after landing

Phase model in `SHM_UpdateCameraSignals`: 0 grounded, 1 ascend (v_z > 30), 2 apex, 3 descend, 4 contact, 5 landing
recovery (`CamPhase`, telemetry `cph`). A critically damped recovery weight (`CamRecoverWeight`, omega = 6 / landing
recover time) starts at contact; the post-contact height rate is the profile's land response plus a severity-scaled
boost (`CamLandingResponseBoost` 12 x (0.5 + 0.5 severity) x weight) through the new `GR_SHM_LandResponse` consumed by
`CT_UpdateCameraHeight`. Committed descent (v_z < -150 cm/s) uses its own follow 0.85 / lag 10 / response 40 with full
weight so the camera arrives at contact instead of hanging. The apex arc is untouched.

## 10. Performance

Whole SHME tick, suite 10 at 30 FPS (self-profiled): **0.54 ms avg / 0.62 ms P95 / 2.8 ms max** (V1.1 0.44 / 0.53).
Split: state + body + gait 0.30 ms, planner + foot plan + execution + settle 0.17 ms, environment 0.04, camera 0.01.
The +0.1 ms is the gait/CoP/intent pass (`SHM_UpdateGait`, 316 nodes). Budget (<= 1.0 ms avg) kept. C++ migration
candidates, unchanged priority: `SHM_UpdateBody`, `SHM_UpdateGait`, planner. Report only.
