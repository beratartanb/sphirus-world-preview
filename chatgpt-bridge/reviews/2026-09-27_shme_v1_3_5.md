## 0. Stairs camera: the user failure reproduced before any change

Real house, lower main-stair flight, gameplay camera, 28.6 game-fps sampled captures (`v135rep_stair_*`):

- **walking up** (`v135rep_stair_walk_up`, frame at mid-flight): the frame shows the stair wall and the ceiling beam
  with only the top of the character's hair in the bottom-left corner - the camera stayed at its world height while the
  body climbed beneath it; camera pitch stayed 0 the whole flight (the control pitch), so the loss is height, not tilt.
- **walking / jogging down** (`v135rep_stair_walk_down`, `_jog_down`, with chest / shoulder telemetry): camera Z minus
  chest Z 53-84 cm (flat reference ~35), camera Z minus shoulder Z 20-43, camera-to-chest distance 62-85 cm with only
  30-37 cm of horizontal offset at the top landing and mid-flight (the boom collapsed by the landing wall), opening to
  145-157 cm only at the bottom; i.e. a close, high, near-overhead view of the head for the first half of the descent.

Cause, from the V1.3.4 design: the body-height follow was pelvis-based, attenuated by 70 % of the constrained level
(the enclosed flight reads 0.6-1.0) and offset by a +22 cm "constrained rise"; on the enclosed stair the follow was
therefore mostly off and the rise pushed the pivot up, while the rig's single boom trace collapsed the arm against the
flight wall. V1.3.4's "head elevation within 2 deg of flat" was measured on the QA stairs box (open space), not here.
The V1.3.4 implementation is superseded in this pass (section 3).

## 1. Redirect continuity and bridge vocabulary

**Worst cases** (spec 6, from the V1.3.4 tables): jog F -> B chained `Jog_Fwd > Jog_Fwd_Pivot > Jog_Fwd > Jog_Bwd >
Jog_Bwd_Start > Walk_Bwd > Jog_Rear` across four databases; jog -135 alternated two pivot variants every frame; sprint
F -> B ran `Sprint_Fwd > Run_Pivot_F_B > Run_Pivot_B_F > Walk_Bwd > Run_Rear`. A human does one thing: plant, pivot on the
loaded foot, push off backward. The vocabulary already exists in project-owned clips (`A_CP2_*` pivots, `A_LR_Run_Pivot_*`,
`A_RB_*` rear loops, `A_CT_*` loops, `af_Loco_Jog_Bwd_Start`) plus four vendor sprint-turn clips; the problem was that
each lived in its own database, so every phase of the event was a database change with the continuing pose invalidated.

**Bridge databases** (`build_redirect_bridges.py`, project-owned, schema `PSS_Default`, no normalization set like the
project's pivot databases; BranchIn tracks on project clips, vendor turn clips duplicated to
`/Game/GraceRanger/SHME/Animations/Redirect/SHM_*`): `PSD_SHM_Redirect_JogReversal` (7: fwd pivots, jog bwd, rear
loops, jog fwd), `_JogLateral` (8), `_SprintReversal` (10: run pivots F->B / B->F both feet, rear loops, sprint fwd,
`SHM_M_Neutral_Sprint_Turn_L/R_180`), `_SprintLateral` (9). Each database holds the WHOLE event, so the search continues
from the pivot into the new-direction loop inside one database.

**Routing** (`install_redirect_bridge.py` + planner): the episode latches its class at onset (reversal if the velocity /
desired angle > `RedirectReversalClassAngle` 120 deg, else lateral; gait sprint above `RedirectSprintSpeed` 340 cm/s;
none below `RedirectBridgeMinSpeed` 200: walks keep the V1.3.4 hold only). On the episode's first frame the node searches
the bridge database (`InterruptOnDatabaseChange`, continuing pose kept as a candidate) and the V1.3.4 hold then keeps it
(database + search) until the episode ends. Two fixes were needed before it held: the alignment exit now requires speed
> 80 cm/s (the velocity direction of a near-stopped body is noise and ended episodes mid-pivot), and the hold / bridge
tolerate the hard-turn and reversal families (2, 3), which fire during a pivot and used to release the hold
(`rewire_redirect_family.py`).

## 3. House-stair shoulder follow

**Anchor** (spec 14-16): the camera pivot now follows the chest (`spine_03`) height relative to the capsule, minus its
rest height (`CamChestRestOffset` 21 cm, calibrated from the flat readings), critically damped at 5 rad/s, grounded only,
with NO attenuation in constrained space and NO constrained-space rise (`CamConstrainedRise` 0: vertical lift is no
longer used as the narrow-space escape). Bound: the follow is limited to 35 cm (`CamBodyMax`), because on this steep
flight the skeleton hangs 40-60 cm below the capsule (foot placement reaches the lower tread) and a pivot that chased the
hips all the way put the boom's rear trace inside the lower steps (first chain-C build, dark frames).

**Shoulder-relationship metrics** (`stair_cam.py`: camera Z minus chest Z and minus shoulder Z, camera-to-chest
distance, horizontal offset; quantiles over the moving frames; flat reference camera Z - chest Z ~35, camera at the

Before, the camera rode 60-70 cm above the chest (36 above the shoulder) through the descent; with the chest anchor it
holds 28-35 cm above the chest, i.e. the flat relationship, and the camera climbs and descends with the body (chest
climb +116 / +154 / -171 cm followed). What remains is the closeness: on the enclosed flight the rig's boom trace still
contracts to 40-105 cm, so the camera sits at shoulder height but close behind the head (section 5). Camera pitch
stays 0 throughout (control pitch), so there is no progressive pitch-down; the "looking down at the head" of the
reproduction was height, now removed. Visual confirmation through the gameplay camera (final build, `stair_walk_up_AFTER.png`, `stair_walk_down_AFTER.png`
beside this report; before: `stair_walk_up_BEFORE.png`): climbing, the frame holds the head and shoulders with the flight
ahead and the camera behind at shoulder height; descending, head and upper torso at the left with the room in depth
below. The 'wall and the top of the hair' frame of the reproduction is gone. What the frames also show: the camera is
close on the flight (torso cut at the frame bottom), the distance / pitch item of section 5.

## 4. Cinematic lens A/B, interior vs exterior distance, depth

**Openness** (spec 23-24): a continuous signal from SHME's existing clearance sweeps, keyed on overhead clearance
(sweep length raised 220 -> 600 cm; `CamOpenness` = clamp((up - 300) / 250) x clamp((min side / rear - 150) / 150)):
0 under any ceiling (living room, workshop, stair), 1 on the lawn and in the forest; it blends through doorways with the
sweep filters. It drives the boom (`CamOpenBoomBonus` 0.38 on top of the profile arms 155 / 172 / 184 and the intensity
term), the shoulder (`CamOpenShoulderScale` 0.7 outdoors, the profile's 40 cm indoors) and a small FOV term through a new
rig pipe (`GR_SHM_FOVDelta` added to `SetFieldOfView`, `install_camera_v135.py`; walk -2 deg, sprint +1 deg outdoors).
A first version keyed on side clearance read a large room as "open" (boom 1.32 in the workshop) and was replaced.

## 5. Narrow-space collision, doorway

**Doorway** (spec 46): openness blends through the veranda door in both directions with no step in the boom -
out: openness 0 -> 0.58 over the veranda, boom mean 1.00, camera-to-chest 85-127 cm, 0 frames under 70 cm; in: 1.0 ->
0 across the porch and the door, boom 1.12 mean, 213 of 2368 frames under 70 cm on the porch steps (the riser, as in the
closure pass), 0 at the door itself. The V1.3.4 doorway result is preserved; the V1.3.4 constrained-space rise is gone.

**Narrow stair, real rig** (spec 41-45): the rig's collision owner is a single sphere trace from the pivot to the desired
camera position (`CameraProbeRadius` 14 cm, camera channel) whose hit distance sets `GR_CollisionArm`, interpolated back
at `CameraRecovery` 5 / s; the spring arm's own collision test is not used; a separate trace clamps the shoulder offset
(`GR_ShoulderClearance`). No multi-axis alternative exists in the rig, and this pass did NOT add one to the rig graph:
what was added is a lateral escape through the pivot offset SHME already feeds the rig (`CamSideShift`, up to 40 cm
toward the side with more clearance, triggered by the rear sweep or by the rig's own boom contraction read back from the
spring arm), so the rig's trace starts beside the character instead of behind the head. On the house flight it did not
fire: the horizontal rear sweep reads 250-395 cm along the flight and the arm-length contraction read 0 (the rig keeps the
indoor arm; the camera's 60-100 cm closeness on the flight is the indoor arm 145 x the narrow reduction, not a collision
collapse). Result on the flight (chain G): camera at shoulder height, head and shoulders framed, torso cut at the frame
bottom; no overhead view, no head-only-from-above; the remaining defect is distance / pitch (an indoor arm of ~130 cm at
pitch 0 puts the chest 29 deg below the view axis), which needs the rig's pitch or arm logic, not touched. Spec 41-43
therefore remain PARTIAL: values audited, no rig graph change.
