## 1. Recovery vocabulary (found / created)

Audit (project + AAMS, no rollback folders): no dedicated recovery-step, brake-and-replant or start-transfer clips exist.
The AAMS start families are the right raw material: every `M_Neutral_Walk_Start_<dir>_<foot>` / `M_Neutral_Run_Start_*`
begins from a stand, loads the support foot, swings the other foot and plants it under the moving COM - a recovery
step is exactly that first step without the walk that follows.

Created (all under `/Game/GraceRanger/SHME/Animations/Recovery/`, sources measured frame by frame with
`analyze_starts.py`, first-step landing = first ball lift > 7 cm then < 3 cm):

| Family | Clips | Source -> owned copy | Hold from | Step travel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MINOR_RECOVERY | 14 `SHM_Rec_<dir>_<foot>` (F, FL, FR, LL, LR, RL, RR, B, BL per first foot) | AAMS walk starts | 2 frames after the first plant (0.37-0.87 s) | 35-107 cm root travel at the plant |
| STRONG_RECOVERY | 16 `SHM_RecS_<dir>_<foot>` (B, BL, BR, FR, LL, LR, RL, RR per first foot) | AAMS run starts | 2 frames after the first plant (0.37-0.57 s) | 36-100 cm |

Reconstruction: root + pelvis + legs held in local space from the hold frame (the clip ENDS planted; upper body keeps
the source settle); inherited PoseSearch notifies stripped; `PoseSearchBranchIn` into the new project-owned

travels > 120 cm (a second step, e.g. walk starts F_L in 1.4 s / 190 cm) were skipped, not forced. Vendor clips
untouched (hash-verified). Metadata manifest (InitialSupport, PlantFoot, FinalSupport, PrimaryStepDirection,
ExpectedTravel, EntryGait/ExitGait, RecoveryStrength, CanMirror): `Saved/Codex/SHME_V13_20260926/recovery_manifest.json`.
Not done: BrakeReplant_L/R as separate clips (the V1.2 corrected run/jog stop copies already are brake-and-replant
motions and stay the STOP family), StartTransfer clips (section 7 solves the start with selection, not a new clip).

## 2. PhysicalMotionIntent routing (runtime path)

```
SHM_UpdateRouting (component, PostPhysics, after SHM_UpdateExecution)
   Blocked      = intent && grounded && sphere sweep along DesiredDir hits within BlockedProbe 55 cm && speed < 25 (0.08 s)
   recovery     = RecoveryDemand > RouteRecoveryDemand 0.45 && FootTargetConfidence > 0.5 && speed < 260
                  && support gate (double support > 0.5 or swing-foot load < 0.45) && the planned step is not blocked
                  (sweep support -> FootTarget) -> family 1 (minor) or 6 (strong, speed > 140)
   hard turn    = intent && speed > 250 && turn angle > 60 && |L_z| > RouteHardTurnAngMom 4 kg m^2/s -> family 2
   reversal     = ReversalDemand > ExecReversal -> family 3 (CP layer owns the motion)
   Family state machine: RouteMinHold 0.35 s before leaving a family, RouteCooldown 0.6 s before re-entering recovery,
   blocked overrides immediately. RecoveryDirLocal/Distance/Side/Urgency = FootTarget - SupportCenter in the facing
   frame, SwingSide, StepUrgency. StanceClass 0 neutral / 1 wide / 2 staggered / 3 recovery.
        |
ABP SHM_Snapshot (game thread, end of BlueprintUpdateAnimation): SHM_Family, SHM_Blocked
        |
Update_MotionMatching entry (before the V1.1 hold branch and the chooser):
   family 1|6 -> SetDatabaseToSearch(PSD_SHM_Recovery, InterruptOnDatabaseChange)
   family 2 && Speed2D > 430 -> PSD_Dense_Stand_Sprint_Pivots ; family 2 -> Integrated PSD_Dense_Stand_Run_Pivots
   else -> unchanged flow (chooser CHT_PBI_PlayerDense etc.)
```
Motion Matching still chooses the detailed pose inside the family (direction, first foot). No PoseSearch schema change.
Flag `MotionRoutingEnabled`; every threshold is on `DA_SHM_Response_Default` (`Route*`, `Blocked*`, `Stance*`).

## 3. Capture-point consumer

The capture point drives RecoveryDemand / FootTarget / SwingSide / StepUrgency (V1.1) and now the recovery family
(`Family` 1/6, telemetry `fam`). Measured: capture error stays <= 1 cm and RecoveryDemand ~0 in every unperturbed
harness stage; it rises only in sprint reversals (0.77 for 2 frames) and sprint wall impacts (1.0, where the planned
step points into the wall and is refused). No harness scenario perturbs balance enough for a legitimate recovery step
- with unperturbed injected input a human would not recovery-step either.

**Database route status:** the family -> `PSD_SHM_Recovery` routing is wired and compiles, the database index builds
(`BuildIndex Succeeded, stored to DDC` once the asset editor touched it; the async build had been cancelled at creation
and PIE searches were "skipped ... AsyncBuildIndex in progress or failed" until then), but in PIE the routed search
still resolves to no selection ('None' for every routed frame, feet static) in three forced runs. Cause not isolated in
this pass (candidates: the duplicated database still carrying the 46 walk-stop entries of its seed - the PoseSearch
entry list is not writable from Python - or the early-return before the rest of `Update_MotionMatching`). The route is
therefore **gated off by default** (`RouteRecoveryToDatabase=false`); detection, telemetry and the vocabulary are live.

## 4. Support-side consumer

- Recovery eligibility requires the swing foot to be unloaded (SupportLoad of the swing side < 0.45) or double support.
- Held-stance starts: the CP start-foot capture (`CP_CaptureStartFoot`) already chooses the first foot from
  `CP_TC_Support`; SHME now writes `CP_TC_Support` = SupportLoadR - SupportLoadL (and raises its confidence) whenever
  the load difference is clear (> 0.3), so the loaded foot stays planted and the unloaded foot steps first.

## 5. Turn selection (walk / jog / sprint 45 / 90 / 180)

Measured: in this project's strafe-based control the QA 90/180 cases are strafe pivots and travel reversals, not yaw
turns - yaw angular momentum stays below 1 kg m^2/s in every suite-5 stage (|L_z| reaches 9-23 only in the wall
suite's turn-aways). Physical turn demand therefore expresses as braking impulse + reversal demand (family 3 fires on
every sprint 180, 11-17 frames per approach), while the L_z hard-turn route (family 2) is implemented and exposed but
inert in these scenarios. Turn vocabulary present: stand turns 45-180 L/R, walk/run/sprint pivot databases, CP2 jog
pivots (`A_CP2_Jog_Fwd/Left_Pivot`), run reversal pivots (`A_LR_Run_Pivot_F_B/B_F`). Gaps: no jog 135 family, no
support-side variants for the CP2 pivots. PBI left at current gains (V1.2 A/B: no visible effect).

## 6. Wall-push regression: root cause and result

Root cause (ABP telemetry `SHMABP`, wall suite): after a sprint into a 45 deg wall the idle takeover latched a stance
snapshot at the impact (speed 490 -> 0 in 0.1 s, HasVelocity false), the input stayed held so Motion Matching kept
playing start/turn attempts at zero velocity; the takeover itself was inactive because the ABP `MovementState` was not
Idle (a term SHME did not track), so no new entry edge happened; at release the takeover re-engaged with the stale
snapshot and dragged the planted foot 38 cm to the impact stance. Fix: (a) the entry edge now uses every takeover term
(CP_IdleReady, HasVelocity, IsFalling, core brake, landing hold, **MovementState == Idle**); (b) the **blocked**
context (intent into an obstacle without progress) suppresses the takeover and the latch, so a wall push is never a
settled idle; (c) V1.2's stricter variants (foot motion under hold, rest dwell) were measured and rejected (they
re-enter mid-step on starts and taps).

|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Held stance -> next start

Before (V1.2): starts from a held stance cost +2.6 cm on the -45/+45 start setups (5.0 -> 7.6). V1.3 routes SHME's
support load into the CP start-foot capture (section 4), so the unloaded foot leaves first. Measured (suite 10 setups,
V1.1 / V1.2 / V1.3): forward 8.0-5.0 / 8.0-5.0 / 8.0-5.6; diagonals 7.4-5.0 / 7.6-7.7 / 7.4-7.7 - no measurable change:
the AAMS start clips still begin from the canonical stance and FootPlacement's lock of the support foot already
hides most of the offset; the remaining +2.6 cm is the swing foot's crossfade from its planted place to the clip's first
plant. A true start-transfer clip (load, release, first step from an arbitrary stance) was not authored in this pass
(gap); the selection consumer is in place for it.

## 8. Backward / wall retreat

`wall_retreat` (suite 3, 67 cm baseline / 65 V1.1 / 68 V1.2) is a backpedal away from a wall at 245 cm/s: the
"slide" window is the backward jog start (`af_Loco_Jog_Bwd_Start_AAMS`) whose foot-speed curves read 0 while the feet
legitimately move with the capsule - the metric counts locomotion, not skating. A curve-independent check (foot world
travel vs capsule travel while the foot is within 6 cm of the ground) is the right instrument; it was not built in this
pass (gap). No optimisation was attempted against the proxy.

6. Recovery clips inspected by frame data only, not at 1x; no BrakeReplant/StartTransfer clips; metadata in a JSON
