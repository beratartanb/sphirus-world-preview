# SPHIRUS Human Motion Engine: V1 report (2026-09-25)

**Acceptance: PARTIAL SHME V1 ACCEPTANCE.**

SHME V1 is implemented, compiles, and runs in the live pawn with every subsystem switchable. A/B evidence shows:
- no regression in any protected row;
- a measured apex-framing fix;
- continuous corridor adaptation with less distance pumping;
- a physically graded turn/brake/reversal/landing state.

It is not FULL:
- There was no player-camera visual review at 1×.
- There was no physical keyboard/mouse/gamepad play and no real 60 FPS run.

- Body coupling and foot planning are not yet proven to look better.

## 3. Mathematics (implemented; details in the skill's `biomechanics.md`)

- **Filters:** all use time-aware `alpha = 1 − exp(−dt/τ)`.
- **Centre of mass:** `COM = Σ mᵢ rᵢ / Σ mᵢ` over 16 segments. Dempster parameters via Winter (2009) Table 4.1, mapped to mannequin bones.
- **Support and stability:** support region is a capsule segment between the feet, weighted by double-support confidence; stability margin = radius − distance(projected COM, region).
- **Capture point (LIP):** `ω = √(g/h)`, `CP = pCOM + v_COM/ω`. Confidence is 0 when airborne or when the COM is too low.
- **Direction-change demand:** `dv = v_des − v`, normalised by `T·μg` into braking, turn, reversal and traction terms. Measured `a_lat = v·ω`. Braking distance `v²/(2μg)`.
- **Landing:** impact = −min vz in the air; severity is mapped between soft and hard impact speeds, with a small horizontal term. Impulse proxy `J ≈ M·Δv`.
- **Environment:** 4 sphere probes in camera yaw space, 2 per frame. Clearances shrink fast and grow slowly; constrained level uses an asymmetric filter plus a Schmitt latch (enter 0.6 / exit 0.4).
- **Camera signals:** critically damped springs `x'' = ω²(t − x) − 2ωx'`, semi-implicit Euler with dt ≤ 0.05.

## 4. What is deliberately approximate

- COM uses animation bones on the source mesh, not a physics body.
- Contact comes from bone speed/height hysteresis, not force sensing.
- The capture point assumes level ground.
- Demand normalisation (μ = 0.8, T = 1 s) is a gameplay heuristic.
- The impulse is a proxy.
- Environment "confidence" is a heuristic.
- None of this is a musculoskeletal or ragdoll simulation.
