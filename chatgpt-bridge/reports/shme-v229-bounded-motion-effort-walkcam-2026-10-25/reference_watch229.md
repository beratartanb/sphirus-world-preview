Not found in the watched segments: a sprint reversal, a stop from a run, or a first step from standing in good light. Those targets stay
the project's own (movement-feel.md), not a measured reference.
Take-aways used as STYLE targets (not numbers): (1) walking framing: a stable over-shoulder composition where turns are carried by the
camera yaw and the body keeps its screen place; (2) direction changes are curves through steps, not pivots on the spot, at walk;
(3) gait changes are progressive.
