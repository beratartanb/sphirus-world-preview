# T1b / player camera evidence (2026-10-31, 03:25–03:34 local)

Same running editor as T1 (no restart). Runner jobs:
- `abp_inspect.py` (measure)
- `t1b_cap.py` (measure)
- `pie_cam.py` (pie)

Lock owner `aitool`, released afterwards. Nothing was saved. The dirty package list was the same before and after: only the 2 ABPs.

## Contents

**`shots/`** — 63 editor renders, 1920×1080, named `<view>__<set>_f<k>.png`.
- `warm` = the discarded first shot.
- S1 = after settling.
- S2 = after the camera was moved away and back.
- S3small / S4medium = with an actor temporarily hidden in the editor.
- S5 = after it was unhidden.
- Exact times are in `data/t1b_log.json`, which also has the chosen actors, their projected screen boxes, the hide events and the dirty lists.

**`player_camera/`** — 12 PIE stills through the real GraceRanger camera.
- Pawn `BP_PBI_Player`, camera manager `BP_PCM_GR_Camera`, FOV 60.
- Camera and pawn transforms are in `data/pie_log.json`.

**`heatmaps/`** — |Δ luma| × 8:
- median vs single-frame maps for the static no-change pair;
- the same for the real-change pairs.

**`sheets/`**
- player-camera 2×2 sheets per station;
- real-change heat maps;
- zoomed crops of the hidden actors.

**`data/`**
- `abp_inspect.json` — dirty ABP inspection.
- `abp_autosave_copies.sha1` — hashes of the protected autosave copies. The binaries are not published.
- `t1b_metrics.json` — median vs single frame; noise-source decomposition; per-frame mean luma.
- `t1b_detect_pair.json`, `t1b_detect_rich.json` — the tile z-score detector with two noise models.
- `player_stats.json` — descriptive statistics of the player stills.
- `t1b_log.json`, `pie_log.json`.

**`TABLES_T1B.md`** — every table, generated by `tools/gen_tables_t1b.py`.

## Reproduce
1. `tools/imgconv.ps1 -Mode png2raw -In shots`
2. With Blender 5.2's bundled Python:
   - `t1b_metrics.py <dir>`
   - `t1b_detect.py <dir> pair|rich`
   - `player_stats.py <dir>`
   - `gen_tables_t1b.py <dir> <out.md>`
